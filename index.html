Chandrachud Pati

I am a pre-doctoral researcher in the DREAM Lab at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), advised by Prof. Yogesh Simmhan, under India's National Quantum Mission (NQM). I work on reliable quantum computing: making results from today's noisy, expensive quantum machines trustworthy, from how a circuit is compiled to how a quantum job runs inside an HPC system. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Quantum compilers

The same circuit, compiled by different tools or exported from different SDKs, can come out with different errors and costs. I find which compilation step lost the quality, and measure how much a circuit's compiled cost depends on the SDK it came from.

IEEE QCE 2026 · IEEE HiPC 2026

Quantum measurement and resource estimation

Time on quantum hardware is scarce, so it matters how many runs an answer really needs. I estimate the shots and QPU time a job needs before it runs, and check before dispatch whether a plan can meet its error target at all.

IEEE HiPC 2026 posters

Quantum–HPC systems

Quantum processors are joining HPC centres, including India's under the NQM. I work on the runtime layer between them: scheduling policies that see how a problem decomposes, and qubit placement that accounts for when a device's calibration will next change.
